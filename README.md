# NeighborBridge ATL

**Find Support. Give Back. Build Community.**

NeighborBridge ATL helps Atlanta residents find food, housing, and emergency assistance, and gives neighbors a way to help each other with their time and skills, not just money.

> **Try it:** _live link coming soon_

## Why it exists

Finding help in Atlanta often means jumping between websites, decoding eligibility rules, and guessing which organization to call. NeighborBridge puts resources in one place, explains them in plain language, shows how recently each one was checked, and points you to a clear next step.

## What you can do

**Find support**
1. Choose **Food** or **Housing** on the home page, or browse every category, including **Emergency** help like the Georgia 2-1-1 helpline.
2. Add your ZIP code or neighborhood (optional), like "30303" or "Downtown."
3. Narrow results to places that are **open now** or **verified**, and switch between a list and a map.
4. Open a resource to see its services, eligibility, hours, and a clear next step.
5. Call the provider, get directions, or visit their website in one tap.

**Know what to trust**
Every resource shows a trust label:
- **Verified:** checked recently against an official source.
- **Partner-verified:** confirmed by a partner organization.
- **Needs re-check:** may be out of date, so confirm before you go.

Cards also show when the information was last checked, where it came from, and recent updates from neighbors, like "3 neighbors confirmed open today."

**Help your neighbors stay informed**
- Tell others whether a place was open, closed, running low, or had a long wait.
- Say whether a page was helpful, or report information that's wrong.
- Suggest a food, housing, or emergency resource we're missing.

**Exchange & Contribute**
- **I Need Help:** find neighbors and organizations offering support.
- **I Want to Help:** find requests you can answer, like clothing donations.
- **Volunteer:** pick up shifts with local groups.
- **Give Back:** discover volunteer opportunities with organizations like Atlanta Community Food Bank and Open Hand Atlanta, then sign up on their official site.
- **Post a Listing** to share your own request or offer. Every listing shows safety tips, like meeting in public and never sending money to someone you haven't verified.

## Built with you in mind

- **No account needed.** Browse everything without signing up.
- **Your privacy matters.** We never ask for your name or personal details, and we don't store your searches or location.
- **Works on any phone.** Designed mobile-first, with large buttons and readable text.
- **Accessible.** Works with a keyboard and screen readers.

## Good to know

This is an early prototype built for **Hacklanta 2026**.

- Some resources and listings are **sample data** and are labeled "Demo listing" or "Sample content."
- **Nothing you submit is sent or saved yet.** That includes neighbor updates, feedback, suggestions, new listings, and "Express Interest."
- The map is a simplified sketch of Atlanta, and pin positions are approximate.
- **Always confirm hours and eligibility directly with the provider.** Availability is never guaranteed.

**In an emergency, call 911.** For free help finding local services, call or text **211**.
