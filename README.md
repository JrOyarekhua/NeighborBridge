# NeighborBridge ATL

**Find Support. Give Back. Build Community.**

NeighborBridge ATL helps Atlanta residents find food and housing assistance, and gives neighbors a way to help each other with their time and skills, not just money.

> **Try it:** _live link coming soon_

## Why it exists

Finding help in Atlanta often means jumping between websites, decoding eligibility rules, and guessing which organization to call. NeighborBridge puts food and housing resources in one place, explains them in plain language, and points you to a clear next step.

## What you can do

**Find support**
1. Choose **Food** or **Housing**.
2. Add your ZIP code (optional).
3. Browse nearby resources, then open one to see services, eligibility, and hours.
4. Contact the provider or get directions in one tap.

**Give back**
Browse the **Community Exchange** for neighbors' needs, offers, and skills, like resume reviews, clothing donations, or tutoring.

## Built with you in mind

- **No account needed.** Browse everything without signing up.
- **Your privacy matters.** We never ask for your name or personal details.
- **Works on any phone.** Designed mobile-first, with large buttons and readable text.
- **Accessible.** Works with a keyboard and screen readers.

## Good to know

This is an early prototype built for **Hacklanta 2026**.

- Some listings are **sample data** and are labeled "Demo data" or "Demo listing."
- Community Exchange listings are examples. Tapping "I'm Interested" doesn't send anything yet.
- **Always confirm hours and eligibility directly with the provider.** Availability is never guaranteed.

**In an emergency, call 911.** For free help finding local services, call or text **211**.
